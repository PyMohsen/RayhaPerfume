Iranian province and city names in `iran_provinces_cities.json` were derived
from the `cities-filtered.json` and `provinces.json` datasets in
[sajaddp/list-of-cities-in-Iran](https://github.com/sajaddp/list-of-cities-in-Iran).
The dataset follows official administrative divisions through Solar Hijri
year 1404 and is distributed under GPL-3.0; see the upstream repository for
the license and source details.
